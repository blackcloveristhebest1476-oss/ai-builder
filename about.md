# AI Builder

Select a structure in the editor, press the **AI** button, describe a design, and AI builds it for you.

- Nothing selected: builds a new design near your camera.
- Something selected: adds to it, or redesigns it with **Replace selection**.
- One undo step per build. Supports Claude (Anthropic) and any OpenAI-compatible API, including local Ollama.

**Setup:** open this mod's settings and paste your API key.
